05 / How I work

Product discipline for engineering-heavy data work.

My operating model is simple: find the painful decision, define the metric, prioritize the roadmap, ship the data capability and measure whether people actually use it.

01

Discover the decision

Who uses the data, which decision depends on it, where trust breaks, and what manual work exists today.

02

Define the product metric

SLA, freshness, quality, ownership, adoption and business KPI become part of the product definition.

03

Prioritize the roadmap

Backlog is scored by business value, risk, feasibility, dependency cost and platform leverage.

04

Ship with controls

Data contracts, tests, monitoring, backfill plan and release notes make delivery predictable.

05

Measure adoption

Usage, incidents, MTTR, dashboard trust and removed manual steps prove that the product works.
